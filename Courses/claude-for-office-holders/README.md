# Claude for Office Holders

For people who hold public office, or plan to: state legislators, county commissioners, city
council members and school board members, of any party. How an AI assistant called Claude can
help you read, question, verify and explain, without giving up the judgment and accountability
that come with the office.

Nonpartisan. No technical background needed. Examples use Kansas law; this is not legal advice.

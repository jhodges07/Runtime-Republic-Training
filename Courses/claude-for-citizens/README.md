# Claude for Citizens

For citizens joining from Facebook or X. How ordinary people can use an AI assistant called
Claude to understand their government, follow their tax dollars, check what they read, and
work together online, without meeting at the same time, to advance the ideals of the AGCL.

No technical background needed. If you can send a text message, you can do everything here.

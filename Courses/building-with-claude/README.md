# Building With Claude

How one person, or a group working online, can use Claude to build software, phone apps
and a real business, under clear written rules and human control.

Lessons are written for the ear and become podcast episodes once approved (CONTROL 05).

| # | Lesson | Status | What you learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [From Turing to Claude](01-from-turing-to-claude/lesson.md) | Draft | Where AI came from, and why Claude was built the way it was |
| 02 | [Working With Claude Like an Engineer](02-working-with-claude-like-an-engineer/lesson.md) | Draft | Best practices: context, control documents, verify everything |
| 03 | [Workflows, Cheat Codes and Automations](03-workflows-cheat-codes-and-automations/lesson.md) | Draft | The repeatable moves that turn one person into a studio |
| 04 | [The Business Under the Apps](04-the-business-under-the-apps/lesson.md) | Draft | Getting an LLC current, and consulting for small business owners |
| 05 | [The Asynchronous Republic](05-the-asynchronous-republic/lesson.md) | Draft | GitHub plus Claude group projects, working together asynchronously |
